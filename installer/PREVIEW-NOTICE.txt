CaptureFlow 0.1.0 Preview

Private pre-release evaluation build. Public release approval is BLOCKED.
The application and installer are unsigned. Windows may display reputation warnings.

Close CaptureFlow before installing, upgrading or uninstalling. Setup does not
force recording sessions to stop or automatically launch recording devices.

This package includes the .NET runtime. Visual Studio and a separately installed
.NET SDK are not required. FFmpeg and FFprobe are NOT bundled. For video, MP3
and editor exports, install an approved external pair and configure its ffmpeg.exe
path in Settings > External Tools. WAV recording remains available without FFmpeg.

User settings, SQLite, recovery data and autosaves are stored separately under
LocalAppData\CaptureFlow. Recordings and editor projects stay in user-selected
locations. Installation, upgrade and uninstall do not remove these user files.

Perceptual audio/video synchronization remains pending manual verification.
Clean Windows validation and complete distribution compliance remain pending.
Third-party notice files are included; complete legal compliance is not claimed.
